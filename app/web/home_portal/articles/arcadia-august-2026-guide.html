Arcadia guide · August 21-29

Arcadia has two remaining August outings worth building an evening around.

One garden-concert date and a picnic-ready orchestra finale make Arcadia's remaining August calendar compact but useful. This guide ranks both active dates by distinctiveness, practical value, and how well each can anchor a complete local outing.

1. Arboretum Summer Nights on August 21: best slower garden evening

When: Friday, August 21 from 5-8 p.m. at the LA County Arboretum. Best for: visitors who want a lower-pressure garden evening after the festival weekend.

The August 21 date uses the picnic-friendly Summer Nights format: gates at 5 p.m., music at 6 p.m., and a 7:30 p.m. last entry. Check the live series page for the current performer rather than choosing the date around an unconfirmed lineup.

Practical review: this is the calendar's flexible reset. It works best when the garden itself is part of the appeal. Arrive early, bring compact seating, and choose an Arcadia dinner afterward only if the concert schedule leaves enough time.

Check the official series page, then browse 40 Arcadia listings.

2. Music of the Night: best late-August Broadway plan

When: Saturday, August 29; picnicking begins at 5:30 p.m. and the concert begins at 7 p.m. at the LA County Arboretum. Best for: Andrew Lloyd Webber fans, musical-theater groups, and people who prefer a polished season finale.

The program closes the Pasadena POPS summer season with music associated with Andrew Lloyd Webber and fellow Broadway composers. Its position at the end of the month makes it the strongest Arcadia plan after the night market has passed.

Practical review: reserve before arranging the rest of the evening. Lawn tickets and table seating create different experiences, so compare the current options instead of assuming every ticket supports the same picnic setup or sightline.

Use the official Arboretum schedule or the Visit Pasadena event page for the latest program and ticket path.

Two ready-made Arcadia evenings

  • Garden date: choose one Summer Nights program, enter at 5 p.m. for garden time, and add a simple picnic rather than rushing to a second venue.
  • Big concert occasion: reserve one Pasadena POPS date, compare lawn and table seating, then make the gate-opening picnic part of the experience.

What to know before an Arboretum plan

Each Arboretum event publishes its own gate, parking, entry, picnic, and bag guidance, so do not carry one program's rules into another. Pack sun protection for arrival and a layer for the evening. Metro riders should leave a realistic transfer or walking buffer.

Before you go

  • Confirm tickets or reservations on the linked organizer page.
  • Recheck parking gates, entry rules, and re-entry policy.
  • Bring water, sun protection, and an evening layer.
  • Use a compact lawn setup for garden and city-lawn events.
  • Choose one venue cluster instead of stacking distant plans.
