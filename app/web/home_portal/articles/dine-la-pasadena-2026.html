What the official listing actually shows

Dine LA Restaurant Week runs August 14-28, 2026, with prix-fixe lunch and dinner menus across the county. The official listing starts at $15 menus and then steps through higher lunch and dinner tiers. Pasadena is not a side note in that list: it has enough participating restaurants to build separate lunch, date-night, seafood, steakhouse, sushi, and Old Pasadena walking routes.

The strongest Perk Nation angle is not another generic "restaurant week is coming" article. It is helping people choose from the actual listings, then sending them into local directory pages, maps, and nearby plans before or after the reservation.
