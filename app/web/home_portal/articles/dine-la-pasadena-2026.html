Restaurant picks to start with

Los Angeles: start with A Food Affair Restaurant, A Tí, a.o.c., ABSteak by Chef Akira Back, and ADKT LA when you want the deepest range of cuisines and reservation styles.

Santa Monica: compare 1212 Santa Monica, Bar Xuntos, Benihana, Bludso's BBQ, and BOA Steakhouse when you want a beach-city dinner before or after a Westside plan.

Beverly Hills: Azur Beverly Hills, Bacari Beverly Hills, Beverly Bar, CUT, and Dante are better fits for polished dinners, date nights, and celebration meals.

Pasadena: Agnes Restaurant & Cheesery, Alexander’s Steakhouse, All India Cafe, Bodegon No. 69, and Bone Kettle give the city a strong mix of value, steakhouse, Indian, Peruvian, and Asian options.

West Hollywood: Ardor, BOA Steakhouse, Bottega Louie, Boxwood, and Casa Madera are strong picks when the night includes cocktails, nightlife, or a higher-energy room.

Culver City: Bacari, Bianca, Butterfly, Carasau, and Dear John's make this one of the best city pages for date-night and dinner-with-friends planning.

El Segundo: Fleming's, Fogo de Chao, Jaime! Taqueria, Jame Enoteca, and Little Sister cover steakhouse, Brazilian, Mexican, Italian, and Southeast Asian intent in one compact area.

Arcadia: All India Cafe, Benihana, Blue Magpie, Hipico, and Hollywood Noodle House make Arcadia useful for family meals, Asian dining, and San Gabriel Valley plans.
