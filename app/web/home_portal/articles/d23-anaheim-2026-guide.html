Anaheim fan-event guide · August 8-16

D23 turns Anaheim into a nine-day fan itinerary. Here is how to choose what is worth your time.

The convention is the headline, but the useful plan starts earlier: an Archives exhibition, a public brewery meetup, a free food-hall gathering, baseball, and a resort day build toward the August 14-16 fan event. This guide ranks the week by value and explains the ticket and standby details that can change the shape of your day.

Start here: choose the pass that matches the day you actually want

At this guide's August 5 update, D23 still listed full-day Sunday passes plus Friday, Saturday, and Sunday afternoon-only passes starting at $49. 1. The Ultimate Disney Fan Event: best full-fandom experience

When: August 14-16 at the Anaheim Convention Center, with evening showcases at Honda Center. Best for: fans who want panels, performances, previews, shopping, exhibits, cosplay, and community in one concentrated weekend.

The three-day event ranks first because it carries the deepest program. Newly announced all-day programming includes an Alan Menken concert, a Jonas Brothers podcast appearance, a Percy Jackson and the Olympians preview, the Mousequerade costume contest, and anniversary programs. Do not build the day around one panel alone: pick one priority, one backup in the same area, and one unreserved activity.

Practical review: full-day Convention Center access runs 9 a.m.-8 p.m.; afternoon-only entry starts at 4 p.m. D23 says overnight queuing is not available. For Honda Center showcases, leave at least the organizer's recommended 90-minute transfer window and follow the strict 5-by-9-by-2-inch standard bag limit unless a medical or diaper-bag exception applies.

Review D23's current all-day programming and read the official standby guidance. Use the Anaheim directory for the parts of the day outside the venue.

2. Capturing Life, Creating Character at Muzeo: best longer-window culture stop

When: opens August 8 and runs through November 1. Best for: animation, film-history, costume, and archive fans who want a quieter experience than a convention floor.

The Walt Disney Archives exhibition explores live-action reference in animation through photography, costumes, artwork, and interactive material. It is the most flexible plan in the guide because it stays open well after convention week. D23's August 8 preview is a member event; check Muzeo's public admission and hours separately.

See the official Archives exhibition announcement, then add food or coffee from Anaheim restaurant listings.

3. D23 Night at the Anaheim Packing District: best free social plan

When: August 11. Best for: mixed groups that want music, trivia, and food choices without committing to a convention pass.

D23 describes this as a free event with a DJ and Disney trivia; food is purchased separately, and an optional World Taste ticket adds samples. This ranks above the other lead-in nights for flexibility. Treat the Packing District as a busy food hall: decide whether your group wants the tasting option or individual vendors before arriving.

Confirm the official weeklong event schedule and compare nearby options in the Anaheim directory.

4. D23 Day at Disneyland Resort: best family-centered splurge

When: August 13. Best for: families and park fans who already planned a valid-admission resort day.

The day includes themed moments at Disneyland Resort, with a one-day Disney Jr. Let's Play! Party at Disney California Adventure Park. D23 says commemorative items are available while supplies last and valid park admission is required. The practical recommendation is simple: treat the event extras as a bonus, not a reason to overpack a normal park itinerary.

Read D23's resort-day details. For meals or errands beyond the resort, open Anaheim restaurants or all 35 live Anaheim listings.

5. D23 Night at Angel Stadium: best sports crossover

When: August 12, Angels vs. Texas Rangers. Best for: baseball fans who value a full game plus themed pregame activity.

D23-member tickets include discounted reserved seating, access to D23 areas, and a commemorative gift. The separate stadium giveaway goes only to the first 23,000 entrants and is not guaranteed by the member ticket. Parking is not included, the venue is cashless, and the official page lists current bag limits; review all three before game day.

Check the official game-night ticket and entry notes, then browse Anaheim or nearby Orange listings.

6. D23 Night at Bottle Logic Brewing: best adults-and-friends warmup

When: August 10. Best for: adults who want a lower-pressure fan meetup before the busiest part of the week.

D23 lists bingo, specialty drinks, and an event that is open to everyone without a membership requirement. That makes it a useful social option, but it is still a brewery setting: confirm age, reservation, food, and transportation details on the current event page before bringing a mixed-age group.

Open D23's current Anaheim event calendar and use Perk Nation's Anaheim directory for a meal or rideshare-friendly second stop.

7. The convention-center program: best for panels, archives, shopping, and cosplay

When: August 14-16. Best for: attendees who prefer several smaller discoveries over one high-demand presentation.

The reservation process is closed, so a resilient floor plan matters. Use the official map to group the Premiere, Walt Disney Archives, Backlot, and Hyperion stages with nearby shopping or exhibit time. If a standby line no longer fits the day, switch to a planned backup instead of sacrificing the rest of the schedule.

Use D23's official schedule and map. Save the Southern California August guide for a non-convention backup day.

8. A no-pass Anaheim day: best flexible fallback

When: any day around August 8-16. Best for: companions who are not attending the convention, sold-out-plan recovery, or a lower-cost extension.

Build around Muzeo, the Packing District, or another confirmed city stop, then let the directory solve the practical gaps. Three ready-made plans

  • One convention day: choose one must-see program, one standby backup, a floor block, and an off-site dinner.
  • No convention pass: pair Muzeo with the Packing District or another separately ticketed lead-in event.
  • Family week: use the August 13 resort day as the anchor and keep the Packing District or Muzeo as the lower-pressure companion plan.

Before buying

  • Check whether your pass includes Honda Center access.

  • Treat while-supplies-last giveaways as a bonus.
