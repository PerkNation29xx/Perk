Orange County guide · September 4-6

Orange County has two current Labor Day plans worth building a day around.

Reviewed by Perk Nation Editorial · Updated September 4, 2026 · How we rank and verify recommendations

Orange International Street Fair and Laguna Beach's closing arts weekend are the two time-sensitive choices still active in this guide. They serve different days: Orange is the stronger food-and-music festival, while Laguna Beach is the better pick for art browsing and a coastal break.

1. Orange International Street Fair: best all-around Labor Day plan

When: Friday, September 4 through Sunday, September 6 in the Old Towne Orange Historic Plaza District. Best for: groups that want international food booths, live music, community organizations, and a full street-fair atmosphere.

The fair begins at 5 p.m. Friday and runs from 10 a.m.-10 p.m. Saturday and Sunday. The organizer lists separate main, All-American, and children's stages, while the compact Old Towne footprint makes food and music easy to combine. Friday is the shortest visit; Saturday and Sunday offer the fullest daytime schedules.

Practical review: choose one meeting point outside the densest plaza area, plan for closed streets and a long walk, and make the day food-and-music first rather than trying to sample every booth. Families should note that Children's Street closes before the main fair.

Check the official 2026 dates, hours, and location, then browse 56 Orange listings for another meal, coffee, shopping, or services in the city.

2. Laguna Beach arts season: best closing-weekend culture day

When: the remaining Sawdust Art Festival and Laguna Art-A-Fair programs continue through September 6. Best for: art lovers who want makers, exhibitions, galleries, and coastal time without committing to a large street fair.

Laguna Beach remains the more flexible choice: choose one festival ground, browse at a deliberate pace, and leave room for the coast rather than treating multiple venues as a checklist. Visit Laguna Beach lists Art-A-Fair admission as reusable for the 2026 show and publishes separate free-entry categories, so compare the current rules with the experience your group wants.

Practical review: confirm the final-weekend hours and admission status for the specific venue. Use the trolley where it fits, carry a weather and parking buffer, and do not assume a pass or ticket transfers across every arts organization.

Use the complete Laguna Beach arts guide, confirm Art-A-Fair's current visitor details, and browse 24 Laguna Beach listings.

Two ready-made Orange County days

  • Orange festival day: arrive before the busiest evening period, choose one meeting point outside the densest plaza area, and let the official stage schedule guide the food loop.
  • Laguna arts day: pick one remaining festival ground for a deliberate visit, take a coastal break, and use the trolley if it fits the route.

How to choose quickly

  • Food, music, and community: choose Orange International Street Fair.
  • Art and a coastal break: choose Laguna Beach's closing festival weekend.
