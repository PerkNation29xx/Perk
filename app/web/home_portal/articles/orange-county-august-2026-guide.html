Orange County guide · August 16-September 6

Orange County has nine current late-summer plans worth building a day around.

Today's final OC Fair and D23 sessions, Laguna Beach's overlapping art season, free county-park concerts and movies, community festivals, adaptive surfing, a major fitness expo, and an early Labor Day weekend in Old Towne Orange make the next month unusually varied. This guide ranks the strongest options by distinctiveness, practical value, and how easily each can become a complete local outing.

1. OC Fair finale: best all-around Orange County day

When: today, Sunday, August 16 from 11 a.m.-11 p.m. Best for: mixed-age groups that want rides, exhibits, animals, food, shopping, and live entertainment in one place.

The fair ranks first because no other plan offers as many ways to shape the same day. The official site lists limited daily capacity and requires advance admission, so the first decision is the date rather than the food or ride list. Wednesday and Thursday general admission is listed lower than weekend admission, while children five and younger enter free.

Practical review: buy admission before committing to the drive, then choose only two or three priorities: rides, livestock and exhibitions, a concert, fair food, or shopping. Trying to cover everything creates more walking and line time than value. The fair is closed Monday and Tuesday.

Use the official OC Fair hours, admission, parking, and capacity page, then compare 34 Costa Mesa listings, 56 Orange listings, or 31 Santa Ana listings for food, hotels, and services.

2. D23 Anaheim finale: best fan-event itinerary

When: the final day of the main fan event is today, August 16. Best for: Disney fans who want to combine the Anaheim Convention Center with one separately planned museum, park, or dining stop.

D23 is bigger than one convention-hall schedule. Official programming extends into Anaheim before the main weekend, while passes, reservations, standby access, and separately ticketed experiences do not all work the same way. That makes it the county's most planning-intensive option.

Practical review: use the pass or confirmed reservation as the anchor, then add at most one citywide activity per day. Do not build an itinerary around hoped-for standby access. Recheck the current ticket and reservation language before travel, and keep Disneyland Resort admission separate from D23 credentials.

Open Perk Nation's four-priority D23 Anaheim guide, confirm updates through D23's official ticket and access page, and browse 35 Anaheim listings.

3. Laguna Beach arts season: best choose-your-own culture day

When: Pageant of the Masters runs nightly through September 4; Sawdust Art Festival and Laguna Art-A-Fair continue through September 6. Best for: art lovers who want one major performance or a flexible mix of makers, exhibitions, galleries, and coastal time.

The county's deepest arts cluster earns a top-three rank because several distinct experiences overlap. The Pageant is the singular evening performance. Festival of Arts presents more than 100 Orange County artists and a full August calendar. Sawdust centers Laguna Beach makers, while Art-A-Fair, the museum, galleries, and city programs offer different scales.

Practical review: choose the Pageant first if it is the reason for the trip, then plan backward. The Passport to the Arts covers Festival of Arts, Sawdust, and Art-A-Fair but not the Pageant. Use the free trolley where it fits, and leave a weather and parking buffer rather than treating three festival grounds as a race.

Use the complete eight-plan Laguna Beach arts guide, review the official Festival of Arts August calendar, and browse 24 Laguna Beach listings.

4. OC Parks concerts and movies: best free outdoor nights

When: concerts continue August 20 and 27; Friday movies continue August 21 and 28. Best for: families, date nights, and groups that want a real summer outing without an admission charge.

OC Parks has the best recurring value in the guide. The remaining concert schedule includes Como La Flor at Mile Square Regional Park, Big Bad Voodoo Daddy at the same park, and Dream Like Taylor at Bluff Park at Salt Creek Beach. The remaining cinema schedule moves from Laguna Niguel Regional Park to Irvine Regional Park and Bluff Park.

Practical review: choose by park as much as by performer or film. Events and parking are free, but arrival, seating, weather, food trucks, concessions, and the walk back to the car still shape the night. Bring chairs or blankets and arrive before the listed performance or sunset time.

Check the official OC Parks 2026 concert and cinema schedule, then browse 17 Irvine listings, 12 Fountain Valley listings, or 14 Dana Point listings.

5. Huntington Beach surf and nature: best active coastal mix

When: the next Bolsa Chica Grunion Run is August 29, Life Rolls On is August 22, and the Pier Swim is August 29. Best for: adaptive athletes, volunteers, open-water swimmers, families, and spectators who want more than a passive beach day.

Life Rolls On's adaptive-surf day is the standout community event, with distinct athlete, water-volunteer, land-volunteer, and spectator roles. The half-mile Pier Swim is for trained open-water athletes. Bolsa Chica adds registered late-night nature programs, Junior Rangers, and weekly beach stewardship.

Practical review: read the registration role before arriving and respect working surf, race, and lifeguard zones. Treat a Grunion Run as a guided wildlife program rather than a guaranteed sighting, and settle late-night transportation in advance. Ocean fitness and pool fitness are not interchangeable.

Use the complete six-plan Huntington Beach guide, confirm the adaptive-surf day through Life Rolls On, and browse 43 Huntington Beach listings.

6. Sea Country Festival: best three-day community festival

When: Friday, August 21 through Sunday, August 23. Best for: families and groups that want rides, games, food, and music with no entry charge.

The City of Laguna Niguel lists free festival entry, with separate charges for rides, games, and food. Friday runs from 5-10 p.m.; Saturday from 2-10 p.m.; Sunday from 2-9 p.m. Pre-sale unlimited-ride wristbands are listed through August 20.

Practical review: choose the night before buying a wristband. Friday works best for a shorter after-work visit, while Saturday has the widest time window. Free entry does not mean a free evening once rides and food are added, so set the spend plan before children see the midway.

Use the official City of Laguna Niguel dates, hours, location, and wristband page.

7. La Habra Corn Festival: best long-running local tradition

When: Friday, August 21 through Sunday, August 23; the parade is Saturday at 9:30 a.m. Best for: families that want a traditional community fair with food, rides, live entertainment, shopping, and a parade.

The La Habra Host Lions Club's 77th festival runs at El Centro-Lions Park, with the Saturday parade on La Habra Boulevard. Its value is the combination of a neighborhood-scale festival and a morning parade rather than one giant attraction.

Practical review: parade-first and rides-first are different days. For the parade, arrive before the road crowd and plan a midday break. For rides and food, consider the cooler evening hours. Buy only through the organizer's linked ticket partner and recheck route or parking details close to the weekend.

Confirm the official Corn Festival hours, parade route, and ride-ticket path.

8. TheFitExpo Anaheim: best fitness and wellness weekend

When: Saturday and Sunday, August 29-30 at the Anaheim Convention Center. Best for: strength athletes, martial-arts and functional-fitness fans, wellness shoppers, and people who want competitions, demos, and workouts under one roof.

The organizer lists competitions, seminars, attendee challenges, fitness activations, cooking demos, exhibitors, and special guests. Saturday and Sunday hours are listed from 9:30 a.m.-5 p.m. at Hall B, with the guest list and schedules subject to updates.

Practical review: choose two content priorities before entering: competitions, workouts, wellness education, gear, or guest appearances. Wear shoes for a convention-floor day, read waiver requirements before joining active sessions, and verify whether a featured experience needs more than general admission.

Use the official Anaheim dates, hours, ticket, venue, and planning page, then compare 35 Anaheim listings or the Anaheim fan-event guide for nearby context.

9. Orange International Street Fair: best Labor Day plan to make now

When: Friday, September 4 through Sunday, September 6 in the Old Towne Orange Historic Plaza District. Best for: groups that want international food booths, live music, community organizations, and a full street-fair atmosphere.

The fair begins at 5 p.m. Friday and runs from 10 a.m.-10 p.m. Saturday and Sunday. It sits just beyond August, but booking transportation, choosing a day, and deciding how to meet a group are August decisions. The event's 2026 transition to Orange Plaza Traditions makes the current organizer page especially important.

Practical review: choose one meeting point outside the densest plaza area, plan for closed streets and a long walk, and make the day food-and-music first rather than trying to sample every booth. Families should note that Children's Street closes before the main fair.

Check the official 2026 dates, hours, and location, then browse 56 Orange listings for another meal, coffee, shopping, or services in the city.

Three ready-made Orange County days

  • Fair-first Saturday: reserve OC Fair admission, choose one ride or exhibition zone and one food priority, then use Costa Mesa listings for a quieter stop outside the grounds.
  • Laguna arts day: pick one festival ground for the afternoon, take a coastal break, then attend the Pageant only with a confirmed ticket and return plan.
  • Free parks night: choose the closest remaining OC Parks concert or movie, arrive before the main program, and pair it with an early local meal rather than relying only on concessions.

How to choose quickly

  • One big all-ages day: choose the OC Fair and reserve admission first.
  • Fan convention: choose D23 only after confirming access for the programs that matter.
  • Art and performance: choose Laguna Beach and anchor the night with the Pageant if tickets are available.
  • Free evening: choose the nearest OC Parks concert or movie.
  • Community tradition: choose Sea Country or the Corn Festival.
  • Fitness and wellness: choose TheFitExpo and prioritize the schedule before entering.
  • Labor Day weekend: make the Orange International Street Fair plan before September begins.
