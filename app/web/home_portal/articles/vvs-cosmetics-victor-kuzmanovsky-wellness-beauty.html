In a world where caring for ourselves and the planet matters more than ever.

In a world where caring for ourselves and the planet has become more crucial than ever, the stories of those who dare to make a difference shine with special intensity. Today, we have the pleasure of introducing you to Victor Kuzmanovsky, a 24-year-old young visionary who lives in beautiful Ohrid, Macedonia. Since childhood, Victor has been fascinated by science and chemistry, a calling he has kept despite the obstacles he has faced.

What began as a childhood curiosity has culminated in the creation of his own brand of day and night creams, unique for their innovative and natural formulas. But what truly distinguishes Victor is his unwavering commitment to his community and the environment.

In this interview, we dive into how he has fused his love of chemistry with a natural and organic lifestyle, creating products that not only beautify the skin, but also nourish the spirit and support a more sustainable future. We will talk about the challenges he has overcome, his vision of social responsibility, and the positive impact he wants to leave in the lives of his customers. Welcome, Victor. Get ready to be inspired by his story.

The interview.

What motivated you to follow the path of science and chemistry?

Since I was little, I have always loved science and chemistry. When I began taking care of my skin, I combined my passion for science with my vegan and organic lifestyle.

What has been the biggest challenge you have faced so far?

My biggest challenge has been overcoming my own fears. I am still working on my personal growth, learning from my mistakes and always being there for myself.

What does giving back to the community mean to you?

Giving back to the community is my number one priority. We plan to do it through gifts for our loyal customers, donations to those in need, animal shelters, orphanages, and other good actions.

What impact do you want your products to have?

I want my customers to have clear and healthy skin, feel good about themselves, appreciate nature, and embrace their beauty.

How has Ohrid influenced your commitment to sustainability?

The beautiful nature of Ohrid is a constant source of inspiration. Many of our ingredients come from nature, and we plan to create our own oils in the future.

Who inspires you most?

My sources of inspiration are many. My family, especially my mother, has been my greatest mentor, always supporting me and seeing my potential. My friends also inspire me, because they believe in my ideas. The most important lesson I have learned is that, with faith in God, everything is possible. God is always with me, and with his guidance I believe we can overcome anything.

How do you keep creativity and innovation in the products?

We focus on simplicity, making sure that our products are effective without making them too complicated. The power is in the herbs and oils we choose.

What challenges have you faced in the beauty industry?

Since I am just beginning, the path has not been easy, but I am very passionate about this and I am determined to keep developing my products.

If you were not in beauty, what would you explore?

I would love to explore acting, modeling, or even business. I have always had a passion for business.

How do you want the brand to evolve?

I visualize my brand as a powerful project that helps people with their skin and increases their self-esteem. I want it to become a source of positivity, embracing beauty in all its forms.

Un fundador joven con una historia de belleza guiada por la ciencia.

En un mundo donde cuidar de nosotros mismos y del planeta se ha vuelto mas crucial que nunca, las historias de quienes se atreven a marcar la diferencia brillan con especial intensidad. Hoy presentamos a Victor Kuzmanovsky, un joven visionario de 24 años que vive en Ohrid, Macedonia.

Desde su niñez, Victor ha estado fascinado por la ciencia y la quimica. Lo que comenzo como una curiosidad infantil ha culminado en la creacion de su propia marca de cremas de dia y de noche, unicas por sus formulas innovadoras y naturales.

En esta entrevista, Cristina Castillo se sumerge en como Victor ha fusionado su amor por la quimica con un estilo de vida natural, vegano y organico, creando productos que buscan embellecer la piel, nutrir el espiritu y apoyar un futuro mas sostenible.

La entrevista.

¿Que te motivo a seguir el camino de la ciencia y la quimica?

Desde pequeño, siempre me ha encantado la ciencia y la quimica. Al comenzar a cuidar de mi piel, combine mi pasion por la ciencia con mi estilo de vida vegano y organico.

¿Cual ha sido el mayor reto que has enfrentado en tu vida hasta ahora?

Mi mayor reto ha sido superar mis propios miedos. Aun sigo trabajando en mi crecimiento personal, aprendiendo de mis errores y siempre estando ahi para mi mismo.

¿Que significa para ti la responsabilidad de devolver a la comunidad?

Devolver a la comunidad es mi prioridad numero uno. Planeamos hacerlo a traves de regalos para nuestros clientes leales, donaciones a los necesitados, refugios para animales, orfanatos y otras buenas acciones.

¿Cual es el impacto que deseas lograr en la vida de las personas que utilizan tus productos?

Quiero que mis clientes tengan una piel clara y saludable, se sientan bien consigo mismos, aprecien la naturaleza y abracen su belleza.

¿De que manera ha influido el entorno natural de Ohrid en tu compromiso con la sostenibilidad?

La hermosa naturaleza de Ohrid es una fuente constante de inspiracion. Muchos de nuestros ingredientes provienen de la naturaleza, y planeamos crear nuestros propios aceites en el futuro.

¿Quienes son tus principales fuentes de inspiracion?

Mis fuentes de inspiracion son muchas. Mi familia, especialmente mi madre, ha sido mi mayor mentora, siempre apoyandome y viendo mi potencial. Tambien me inspiran mis amigos, quienes creen en mis ideas. La leccion mas importante que he aprendido es que, con fe en Dios, todo es posible.

¿Como mantienes la creatividad y la innovacion en tus productos?

Nos enfocamos en la simplicidad, asegurandonos de que nuestros productos sean efectivos sin complicarlos demasiado. El poder esta en las hierbas y aceites que elegimos.

¿Que desafios especificos has enfrentado en la industria de la belleza?

Como apenas estoy comenzando, el camino no ha sido facil, pero soy muy apasionado por esto y estoy determinado a seguir desarrollando mis productos.

Si no estuvieras en la industria de la belleza, ¿a que otro ambito te gustaria dedicarte?

Me encantaria explorar la actuacion, el modelaje o incluso los negocios. Siempre he tenido una pasion por el negocio.

¿Como te gustaria que tu marca evolucionara en los proximos años?

Visualizo mi marca como un proyecto poderoso que ayude a las personas con su piel y aumente su autoestima. Quiero que se convierta en una fuente de positividad, abrazando la belleza en todas sus formas.

El cierre de Cristina.

La historia de Victor no solo trata de cuidado de la piel. Tambien habla de vencer miedos, elegir coraje y construir una marca con responsabilidad hacia las personas, los animales y el planeta.
