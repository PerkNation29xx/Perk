Los Angeles fashion guide

LA fashion events to plan around now.

Fashion intent is strongest when it is tied to something timely: a market week, a sample-sale Friday, a warehouse drop, a beauty pop-up, or a night out nearby. This guide turns that demand into a practical Los Angeles itinerary readers can act on today.

Updated July 24, 2026 · Los Angeles · Fashion, shopping, beauty, events

Start with Downtown LA Market Week.

The clearest near-term fashion calendar hook is Downtown LA. California Market Center lists its next market window as August 2-5 for kids' collections and August 3-5 for women's and men's collections, with another market window on October 12-14. The New Mart also lists August 3-6 and October 12-15 market weeks for contemporary apparel and accessories.

Most wholesale market activity is trade-only, so the reader service angle is not "show up and shop every showroom." The better article strategy is a planning guide: where buyers stay, where teams eat nearby, what public-facing sample sales to watch, and how visitors can make a Downtown LA fashion day out of the surrounding district.

Use sample-sale Fridays for public shopping intent.

CMC's public-facing sample-sale calendar is the strongest recurring shopping angle. Its next listed dates are July 31, August 28, September 25, and October 30, with usual hours around 9 AM to 3 PM and some showrooms staying open later. This should become a monthly Perk Nation refresh: update the date, featured floors, nearby coffee/lunch ideas, and internal links to LA Fashion District businesses.

Add warehouse drops and beauty pop-ups as weekend hooks.

LA Vintage is promoting an August 8 warehouse sale, a direct fit for readers searching thrift, vintage, denim, Y2K, and affordable shopping. The Grove's current events calendar also has an EESEOL K-beauty pop-up running July 17-August 15, which broadens the piece beyond apparel and gives fashion readers a beauty-and-shopping plan around Fairfax, Mid-City, and Beverly Grove.

Recommended plan.

  • For buyers: use LA Market Week as the anchor, then add Downtown dining and hotel links.
  • For public shoppers: lead with CMC sample-sale Fridays, LA Vintage, and Fashion District pop-ups.
  • For lifestyle readers: pair beauty pop-ups with dinner, dessert, or events in nearby neighborhoods.
  • For SEO: internally link "Los Angeles fashion events," "LA sample sales," "LA Market Week," and "Fashion District shopping" to this guide and relevant directory searches.
