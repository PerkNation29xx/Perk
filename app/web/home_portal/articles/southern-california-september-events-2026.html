September 2026 event guide

Ten Southern California September plans worth building a day around.

September opens with street food and Labor Day festivals, then moves into fan culture, stadium nights, outdoor art, creative careers, and two very different Santa Monica music plans. These picks are ranked for their range, practical value, and connection to cities with useful Perk Nation directory coverage.

1. Orange International Street Fair: best all-around Labor Day plan

When: September 4-6. Best for: groups that want food, live entertainment, local traditions, and an easy walk through Old Towne Orange.

This ranks first because it works for the widest mix of visitors. Orange Plaza Traditions confirms the three-day 2026 run in the historic Plaza district, while the City of Orange has approved the surrounding street closures. Go earlier for a calmer food-and-crafts loop or later for the livelier music atmosphere, and use the organizer's current map before choosing a meeting point.

Check the Orange Plaza Traditions event page, then build the rest of the day with 56 Orange directory listings.

2. Long Beach Greek Festival: best food-and-culture weekend

When: September 5-7, opening at noon daily. Best for: families and food-focused groups looking for a lower-cost festival with music, dancing, and cultural programming.

Visit Long Beach lists $5 admission at Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary Greek Orthodox Church, plus free parking and continuous shuttle service from the nearby VA Medical Center lot. That published shuttle plan gives this festival an edge over events where parking is left to chance. The city's Labor Day guide also lists Long Beach Comic Con, a waterfront run, and other weekend options if your group wants to split the day.

Use Visit Long Beach's Greek Festival guide, then compare 208 Long Beach businesses.

3. BlizzCon in Anaheim: best fan and gaming weekend

When: September 12-13; on-site registration begins September 11. Best for: Blizzard fans, esports viewers, cosplayers, collectors, and game-industry job seekers.

The official event page confirms esports finals, Community Night, the Darkmoon Faire, portfolio and résumé reviews, and a Sunday closing performance by LE SSERAFIM. This is the guide's highest-commitment convention pick, so secure admission first and then decide whether to collect credentials Friday. The organizer publishes detailed parking, rideshare, public-transit, and accessibility guidance; review it before booking a hotel or committing to drive both days.

Plan with the official BlizzCon event page, then browse 35 Anaheim listings for nearby food, hotels, shopping, and services.

4. Pasadena ARTWalk: best free art-and-maker weekend

When: September 19-20, 11 a.m.-6 p.m. Best for: art shoppers, design-minded visitors, families, and anyone who wants a free daytime event.

Visit Pasadena lists more than 180 artisans and makers on Green Street between Los Robles and El Molino. The free admission and compact Playhouse Village footprint make it easy to browse without treating the event like an all-day endurance test. Start with a shortlist—visual art, photography, jewelry, fashion, or home goods—then leave a meal break instead of trying to see every booth in one pass.

Confirm details with Visit Pasadena, then use 563 Pasadena listings for coffee, lunch, dinner, or services nearby.

5. Ocean Way Festival in Santa Monica: best destination beach concert

When: September 26-27. Best for: music fans who want a full beach-festival weekend rather than a short evening concert.

The City of Santa Monica confirms The Killers and Olivia Dean as headliners for the inaugural two-day event, with Jack White, Khruangbin, Sublime, Durand Jones and the Indications, Alvvays, and more on the announced lineup. The beach setting is the advantage and the planning challenge: confirm the final entry, transit, re-entry, and weather guidance before leaving home, and treat each day as a destination outing.

Read the City of Santa Monica announcement, then browse Santa Monica businesses.

6. UCLA's Rose Bowl home opener: best local college-football day

When: September 12. Best for: Bruins fans, alumni groups, and visitors who want a stadium event with time for a Pasadena meal before or after.

Discover Los Angeles lists UCLA against San Diego State as the Bruins' 2026 home opener. This is easier to enjoy when the transportation plan comes before the restaurant plan: use official team and Rose Bowl updates for kickoff, parking, shuttle, and entry rules, then choose food around the route you are actually taking rather than the neighborhood you hope will be convenient.

Review the current Discover Los Angeles event roundup, then start with the Pasadena directory.

7. Burbank Career Transitions Expo: best creative-industry opportunity

When: September 18, 12:30-4:30 p.m. Best for: media and entertainment professionals navigating a career change or looking for focused networking and practical guidance.

The City of Burbank says this is the first event in a new three-part Media Industry Expo series and that the smaller format is designed for more personalized networking, panels, and workshops. That makes it a better fit for people with a defined career question than a broad entertainment festival. Review participating employers and sessions before registering, then arrive with a short introduction and a specific next-step goal.

Read the City of Burbank announcement, then explore 968 Burbank directory listings.

8. Tongva Twilight in Santa Monica: best free picnic-and-movie night

When: Saturday evenings through September 12, beginning at 7 p.m. Best for: a low-cost date, family picnic, or relaxed outdoor evening near the coast.

The City of Santa Monica describes the six-week Tongva Park series as free live music and open-air cinema. It is the strongest budget alternative to Ocean Way: bring a picnic, plan for cooler evening temperatures, and check the city's calendar for the selected Saturday's program. Because it starts at 7 p.m., it can follow a beach afternoon without turning the entire day into a ticketed festival commitment.

Read the city's Tongva Twilight announcement, then find nearby Santa Monica options.

9. OneRepublic with fireworks at the Hollywood Bowl: best polished concert night

When: September 11-12. Best for: concertgoers who want a major headliner and a fireworks finale in one reserved-seat evening.

Discover Los Angeles lists OneRepublic for the Hollywood Bowl's fireworks finale. This ranks below the free community events on value, but higher on production scale and the simplicity of one clear nighttime plan. Confirm the selected date with the Bowl, use its current venue rules, and decide on shuttle or parking before making dinner reservations.

Check the current Discover Los Angeles listing, then use 61 Los Angeles listings or browse nearby restaurants.

10. The Great Outdoors at Greystone: best Beverly Hills nature-and-design stop

When: September 19. Best for: garden lovers, architecture fans, and visitors who prefer a focused daytime cultural plan to a large street festival.

The City of Beverly Hills describes this as its annual celebration of nature at Greystone. The setting—not scale—is the reason to go: pair the event with time in the grounds and build the rest of the day around a nearby meal or art walk. Check the city's event page for current admission, reservation, and access details before arriving.

Confirm the Greystone program with the city, then browse Beverly Hills businesses.

How to choose quickly

  • Labor Day food and culture: choose Orange International Street Fair or Long Beach Greek Festival.
  • Fan weekend: choose BlizzCon; for football, choose UCLA at the Rose Bowl.
  • Free art day: choose Pasadena ARTWalk.
  • Beach music: choose Ocean Way for a ticketed destination weekend or Tongva Twilight for a free local evening.
  • Career value: choose Burbank's Career Transitions Expo.
  • One polished night: choose the Hollywood Bowl fireworks finale.
  • Quieter culture: choose The Great Outdoors at Greystone.
