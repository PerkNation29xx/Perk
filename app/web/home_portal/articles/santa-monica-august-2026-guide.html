Santa Monica guide · October 19-24

Santa Monica has eight current October plans built around food, theater, art, history, community, movement, swimming, and seasonal storytelling.

Reviewed by Perk Nation Editorial · Updated October 19, 2026 · How we rank and verify recommendations

Scott Silven's Wonders runs October 22-24 at BroadStage, while LA Times Food Bowl fills Barker Hangar with restaurant tastings and a live 101 Best Restaurants reveal on October 23-24. Spooky Splash adds a family pool-and-pumpkin night on October 23. On October 24, the free Pico Walk Loop ribbon cutting and community walk can lead into Ghosts of the Miles' free all-ages festival, the separate evening local-history performance, or the free Held in Trust municipal-art opening at Bergamot Station. Coastal photography and the Pico Farmers Market provide flexible alternatives. The rankings favor distinctiveness, practical value, and how easily each plan extends into a complete Santa Monica day.

1. LA Times Food Bowl: best all-in-one restaurant tasting

When: October 23-24; Friday 7-10 p.m. with 6 p.m. VIP entry. Best for: serious food travelers, restaurant-ranking readers, chef-demo audiences, date nights, and adults who want many Southern California kitchens in one ticket.

The Barker Hangar hosts a two-night, all-inclusive food-and-drink festival with dozens of restaurants, live demonstrations, cookbook signings, and entertainment. Friday's 21-and-older program reveals the Los Angeles Times' 101 Best Restaurants ranking live, while the wider published lineup reaches from Santa Monica and Beverly Hills to Glendale, Long Beach, and the San Gabriel Valley. It ranks first for the concentration of restaurants and chefs, but it is a premium ticketed experience rather than a budget dinner. Confirm the exact night, age rule, ticket tier, transportation plan, and included offerings before buying.

Review the organizer's current Friday schedule, lineup, age rule, venue, and ticket link, compare the two-night Visit Santa Monica overview, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

2. Scott Silven's Wonders: best intimate illusion performance

When: October 22-24, 7:30 p.m.; 75 minutes. Best for: theatergoers, magic and mentalism fans, date nights, teens and adults, and visitors who want a compact indoor performance.

Scott Silven blends illusion, hypnosis, memoir, and audience perception in a three-night BroadStage run. It ranks second because the venue describes a tightly structured 75-minute theater piece rather than a drop-in attraction, making the time commitment clear for dinner planning. Choose the earliest available date if schedule flexibility matters, confirm current seat prices and accessibility with the box office, and arrive with enough time for parking and theater entry rather than planning another major event immediately beforehand.

Check BroadStage's official dates, running time, venue details, and tickets, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

3. Spooky Splash: best family pool-and-pumpkin night

When: October 23, 5-8 p.m. Best for: families, recreational swimmers, pumpkin-patch fans, youth and teens, and groups looking for an active Halloween event rather than a seated performance.

The Santa Monica Swim Center combines recreational swimming, a floating pumpkin patch, an obstacle course, spooky movies, crafts, treats, food trucks, and a haunted house. It ranks ahead of Ghosts of the Miles for broad family activity, though tickets cost $10 for residents and $15 for non-residents, with resident and non-resident family packages at $32 and $48. Buy in advance to reduce entry friction, confirm pool rules and supervision requirements, and pack swim gear plus a dry layer.

Review the City of Santa Monica's official hours, activities, address, and ticket prices, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

4. Ghosts of the Miles: best choose-your-intensity Halloween plan

When: free all-ages Festival of Memories October 24, noon-3 p.m.; ticketed 16+ Historical Haunt October 23-24, starting at 5:30 p.m., with timed sessions through 10:45 p.m. Best for: families with younger children in the daytime, local-history audiences, theatrical-walk fans, Halloween date nights, architecture lovers, and groups choosing between gentle activities and a darker evening.

Panda Roja Design brings a weekend of history and seasonal storytelling to the historic Miles Memorial Playhouse with support from Santa Monica's Art of Recovery initiative. The daytime Festival of Memories is free with RSVP encouraged and invites families to explore the playhouse, try lighthearted Halloween activities, and meet cast members. The evening Historical Haunt is a separate, limited-ticket immersive tour intended for ages 16 and older. This two-format structure is the advantage: families can choose the afternoon without paying for an evening show, while older audiences can reserve a timed performance. Confirm which version fits the group before planning dinner and allow extra time around Lincoln Boulevard traffic and parking.

Review the City of Santa Monica's official free family-festival hours, age guidance, venue, and RSVP note, compare Visit Santa Monica's evening dates and ticket link, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

5. Pico Walk Loop ribbon cutting: best free neighborhood-and-mobility walk

When: October 24, 10 a.m.-noon. Best for: neighborhood walkers, families, mobility advocates, Pico residents, seniors, and visitors who want a free morning activity before another city event.

Santa Monica and Adopt-A-Walk will open the Pico Walk Loop with a ribbon cutting and community walk from Virginia Avenue Park. It ranks below the larger Halloween programs because it is a focused two-hour civic event, but the free admission, broad age range, and clear neighborhood connection make it a useful active start to October 24. Wear comfortable shoes, bring water and sun protection, and use the finish near Virginia Avenue Park to decide whether the Pico market or a later Miles Playhouse program fits the rest of the day.

Review the City of Santa Monica's official date, hours, meeting point, and free admission, explore the city's Walk Loop project, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

6. Held in Trust: best free municipal-art opening

When: October 24, 4-6 p.m. Best for: contemporary-art audiences, civic-art researchers, Bergamot Station visitors, students, and groups wanting a free indoor stop after an outdoor program.

City Gallery gathers works acquired for Santa Monica's municipal Art Bank from the mid-1980s to the present, bringing pieces normally rotated through city facilities into one exhibition. The artist list includes Kim Abeles, Edgar Arceneaux, Jo Ann Callis, John Divola, Jim Isermann, Michael C. McMillen, Ed Moses, Roland Reiss, James Turrell, and others. It ranks below the one-off civic and Halloween programs because the opening is a focused art experience, but that focus and the Bergamot Station setting make it a strong free choice for gallery audiences. Allow 45-90 minutes, confirm access before leaving, and check parking or transit because multiple October 24 events compete for attention across Santa Monica.

Review the City of Santa Monica's official date, hours, location, exhibition context, and artist list, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

7. Portraits: Faces and Waves: best free flexible photography stop

When: October 3-March 27, 2027; daily except special events. Best for: photography audiences, Pier visitors, coastal-history readers, portraiture students, and groups needing a quieter alternative to a timed festival.

The Annenberg Community Beach House Gallery pairs Sam Comen's portraits of 274 Santa Monica Pier visitors with Anthony Friedkin's photographs of waves made near the Beach House and State Beach. One body of work records who came to the Pier during America's 250th year, while the other treats individual waves as short-lived forms. It ranks below the one-day programs because it can be visited later, but that flexibility is its advantage. Admission is free; check same-day gallery access around special events, allow 30-60 minutes, and combine the show with the beach or a nearby meal.

Read the City of Santa Monica's official dates, artist context, location, and access note, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

8. Pico Farmers Market: best repeatable food-first morning

When: Saturdays, 8 a.m.-1 p.m. Best for: produce shoppers, picnic planners, families, and visitors who want a low-pressure neighborhood stop.

The Virginia Avenue Park market combines seasonal produce, flowers, bread, cheese, prepared food, and live music in the Pico neighborhood. It ranks below the one-off cultural programs because it is repeatable, but that also makes it the safest fallback if capacity, weather, or timing disrupts another plan. Go early for shopping range or later for lunch energy, and bring a reusable bag plus a separate budget for prepared food.

Confirm the October 24 Pico Farmers Market listing, then browse more Santa Monica food options.
