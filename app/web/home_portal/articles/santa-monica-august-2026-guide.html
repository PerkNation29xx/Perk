1. Between Darkness and Dawn: best flexible free art stop

When: Thursday-Sunday, noon-5 p.m., through September 27. Best for: contemporary-art viewers, Airport Arts Center visitors, families seeking a short indoor stop, and groups that need a weather-flexible backup.

The Westside Artists Critique Group exhibition at Propeller Gallery is all ages and free, with recurring afternoon hours that make it the easiest plan in the guide to move around. It ranks below the one-night music events because it is smaller and self-paced, but that is also its advantage: allow 45-90 minutes, combine it with one nearby meal or market, and keep it as a backup if crowds or weather change another outdoor plan.

Confirm a current date on Visit Santa Monica's exhibition schedule, then browse Santa Monica businesses.

2. Wellness & Waves: best free start to a coastal Saturday

When: Saturdays through October 17, 8-10 a.m. Best for: beginners, families, visitors without a gym membership, and anyone who wants activity before the Pier becomes busiest.

The Santa Monica Pier Corporation's free, all-ages series rotates accessible workouts in an open-air setting. Its value is the combination of zero admission and an early finish: you can move, cool down, and still reach a market or breakfast before midday. Bring water, sun protection, and a layer for marine air; check the weekly class format so the intensity matches your group.

Confirm the current Wellness & Waves session with Visit Santa Monica, then find a Santa Monica meal.

3. dublab Open Air: best free two-night park soundtrack

When: September 24-25, 5-8 p.m. Best for: jazz and experimental-music listeners, DJ audiences, friend groups, and dancers.

Thursday's Cosmic Sounds program features SML and Liberate Elemental Forces; Friday shifts to dance-floor sets from Spiñorita, GANAS, and ANTHNY GBRIEL at Christine Emerson Reed Park. Choose Thursday for a more exploratory live-listening night or Friday for movement and party energy. Arrive near the opening for an easier meeting point, bring a layer for the evening, and verify any weather or program update before traveling.

Check the city's current dublab Open Air listings, then use Santa Monica restaurant search for dinner before or after.

4. Doors Open California: best free architecture-and-history visit

When: September 26-27, noon-4 p.m. Best for: architecture fans, Old Hollywood history readers, families with older children, and visitors who want a focused afternoon stop.

Free docent-led tours open the Marion Davies Guest House and introduce Julia Morgan's design, the former beachfront estate, and artifacts that are not usually presented together. No reservation is required, which makes this easier to combine with the beach than a timed museum ticket. The city also suggests the Santa Monica Conservancy's Shotgun House as a second history stop; treat that as an optional two-mile add-on rather than assuming the sites are adjacent.

Review the official Doors Open California event page, then compare nearby Santa Monica options.

5. Pico Farmers Market: best repeatable food-first morning

When: Saturdays, 8 a.m.-1 p.m. Best for: produce shoppers, picnic planners, families, and visitors who want a low-pressure neighborhood stop.

The Virginia Avenue Park market combines seasonal produce, flowers, bread, cheese, prepared food, and live music in the Pico neighborhood. It ranks below the one-off cultural programs because it is repeatable, but that also makes it the safest fallback if capacity, weather, or timing disrupts another plan. Go early for shopping range or later for lunch energy, and bring a reusable bag plus a separate budget for prepared food.

Confirm the current Pico Farmers Market listing, then browse more Santa Monica food options.

Important Ocean Way update

The Ocean Way Festival scheduled for September 26-27 has been canceled after storm surf and runoff damaged the beach site. Do not travel to Santa Monica for the festival. The city says the inaugural event is now planned for 2027; ticket holders should follow the organizer's refund instructions.

Read the City of Santa Monica's cancellation notice.

Build a practical Santa Monica day

For September 24-25, build the evening around the dublab program rather than trying both nights. For September 26-27, use the Guest House tour as the anchor and add Wellness & Waves, the Pico market, the gallery, or the Shotgun House only if travel time suits the group. Before you go

  • Confirm times and weather on the linked official page.
  • Bring water, sun protection, and a coastal layer.
  • Check Metro E Line service or parking before evening programs.
