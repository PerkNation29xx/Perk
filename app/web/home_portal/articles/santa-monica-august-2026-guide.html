1. Cinema by the Sea: best Saturday outdoor-movie date

When: Saturday evenings from 8-11 p.m. at Regent Santa Monica Beach through August 29. Best for: couples, film fans, and visitors who want one planned evening near the ocean without building a full festival day.

The remaining August film is Grease on August 29. The hotel pairs the Route 66-themed series with cocktails and food, making the specific film and the venue experience equally important.

Practical review: choose by movie first. Confirm the current reservation or seating path, food-and-beverage expectations, age rating, and weather policy before making dinner plans. This is a hospitality-led screening, not a bring-anything public-park movie.

Check Visit Santa Monica's August 29 Cinema by the Sea listing.

2. Wellness & Waves: best free active morning

When: Saturdays from 8-10 a.m. at the Santa Monica Pier through October 17. Best for: beginners, families, and weekend visitors who want movement before the beach and promenade become busiest.

Visit Santa Monica lists the Pier program as free, all ages, and open without a membership. Its value is simplicity: the session creates a reason to arrive early, then leaves the rest of the day open for the beach, Palisades Park, or Downtown.

Practical review: check the Pier's current instructor schedule, bring water and the equipment named for that session, and arrive with enough time to find the west-end program area. Coastal mornings can be cool even on hot inland days.

Confirm the schedule through Visit Santa Monica's August 29 Wellness & Waves listing and the linked Santa Monica Pier schedule.

3. A Talk Through Pier History: best free local-history hour

When: Thursday, August 27 from 6-7 p.m. in the historic Merry Go-Round Building. Best for: architecture, Route 66, and local-history fans who prefer conversation to a self-guided attraction.

Santa Monica Pier Executive Director and historian Jim Harris leads the one-hour storytelling session and Q&A. Admission is listed as free, but seating is limited. The timing is the advantage: the talk ends early enough to continue into sunset and dinner.

Practical review: reserve or verify entry before arriving, then keep the rest of the evening flexible. This ranks above a standard Pier walk because the live historian and Q&A offer context that the landmark alone does not.

Review Visit Santa Monica's Pier history listing.

4. Downtown Farmers Market: best food-first morning

When: Wednesday and Saturday mornings, with the Downtown calendar listing August dates throughout the month. Best for: home cooks, produce-focused visitors, and people who want a local morning before the Pier or beach.

Downtown Santa Monica describes the market as a major source of locally grown produce, and its visitor map lists the Wednesday and Saturday market window as 8 a.m.-1 p.m. The market works best as a purpose-driven stop rather than a generic stroll: arrive with a short shopping list, then leave room for something seasonal.

Practical review: Wednesday is the more serious food-shopping choice; Saturday is easier to pair with Wellness & Waves. Bring a reusable bag, expect market-day loading and parking constraints, and avoid buying temperature-sensitive items before a long beach day.

Check the Downtown Santa Monica event calendar, then use Perk Nation to find restaurants or browse shopping.

5. Ocean Way Festival: best September plan to decide in August

When: Saturday and Sunday, September 26-27 on Santa Monica Beach. Best for: music fans choosing one destination weekend rather than several smaller August events.

The inaugural Goldenvoice festival is headlined by The Killers and Olivia Dean, with Jack White, Khruangbin, Sublime, Durand Jones and the Indications, Alvvays, SG Lewis, Hiatus Kaiyote, Poolside, Hot Chip DJs, and more. The city lists two stages, open blanket areas, and a location between the Pier and Bay Street.

Practical review: this is the expensive decision in the guide. The city's announcement listed two-day general admission starting at $399 and premium-pit access starting at $749 when sales opened; verify live pricing and inventory before buying. There is no on-site parking by design, so Metro E Line, Big Blue Bus, shuttles, biking, or another car-free approach should be part of the ticket decision.

Read the City of Santa Monica's Ocean Way announcement and follow its official ticket link.

Three ready-made Santa Monica days

  • Free coastal Saturday: start with Wellness & Waves, add a Pier or Palisades Park walk, and leave the evening open unless the selected outdoor movie is worth a second commitment.
  • Pier history evening: use the free August 27 talk as the anchor, then keep sunset and dinner flexible.
  • Market and movie: shop the Saturday farmers market, take a midday break, then return only if the selected Cinema by the Sea film justifies a second trip.

What to know before a Santa Monica event day

Coastal traffic and event closures can turn a short map distance into a slow arrival. For Pier, Palisades Park, Downtown, and beach events, compare the Metro E Line, Big Blue Bus, biking, and rideshare before choosing parking. Bring a layer for night programs, check oceanfront weather rather than inland temperatures, and confirm whether the venue allows outside food, blankets, chairs, or re-entry.

Before you go

  • Confirm reservations, admission, and the selected film or instructor.
  • Recheck coastal weather and the city's current street closures.
  • Use transit for Pier, Downtown, and major beach-festival plans when possible.
  • Bring a layer for pool, movie, and Pier programs after sunset.
  • Do not assume every venue permits outside food, chairs, or re-entry.
