Santa Monica guide · November 7-March 20

Santa Monica has six current plans built around independent makers, civic celebration, coastal art, holiday shopping, and local food.

Reviewed by Perk Nation Editorial · Updated November 3, 2026 · How we rank and verify recommendations

Renegade Craft brings more than 275 independent artists to Santa Monica Airport on November 7-8, followed by the city's November 11 Veterans Day ceremony and the first Swiss Winter Village at the Pier from November 18 to January 3. Santa Monica City Gallery's Held in Trust, Portraits: Faces and Waves, and the Pico Farmers Market provide flexible alternatives. The rankings favor distinctiveness, practical value, and how easily each plan extends into a complete Santa Monica day.

1. Renegade Craft at Santa Monica Airport: best independent-maker weekend

When: November 7-8, 10 a.m.-4 p.m. Best for: holiday shoppers, independent-art supporters, design fans, families, food-truck visitors, and groups looking for an outdoor market with a broad maker roster.

Renegade brings more than 275 curated artists to the Santa Monica Airport Interim Open Space for two days of small-business shopping. The mix of established and emerging makers makes this a stronger discovery stop than a conventional mall trip, while food trucks and the outdoor layout give groups room to split up and compare finds. Entry is a suggested $5 contribution, parking is free, and the fair runs rain or shine. Wear shoes for a concrete surface, bring sun or weather protection, and use carpooling, biking, transit, or rideshare if the main lot fills.

2. Santa Monica Veterans Day ceremony: best free beachside civic program

When: November 11, 11 a.m.-1 p.m. Best for: veterans, military families, civic-program audiences, live-music listeners, aviation enthusiasts, and groups seeking a free public remembrance.

Santa Monica and the U.S. Army move the city's seventh annual ceremony to Beach Parking Lot 4 between Santa Monica Beach and Crescent Bay Park. The 11 a.m.-noon ceremony includes military and city speakers, the 300th Army Band, a future-soldier swearing-in, and a planned Chinook/C-130 flyover; music and entertainment continue until 1 p.m. Arrive before 11 a.m., bring a coastal layer, expect amplified sound, and confirm parking or transit before traveling.

Review the City of Santa Monica's official location, schedule, program, and military-benefit details, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

3. Held in Trust at Santa Monica City Gallery: best free flexible local-art survey

When: October 24-March 20, 2027; Wednesday-Saturday, 11 a.m.-5 p.m., except city holidays. Best for: Southern California art audiences, gallery newcomers, architecture and design fans, and groups needing a free indoor plan with a long booking window.

Santa Monica gathers 16 artists from its Art Bank Collection for the first time in one exhibition, spanning acquisitions from the 1980s to the present. The roster includes Kim Abeles, Edgar Arceneaux, Jo Ann Callis, John Divola, Charles Garabedian, Jim Isermann, Rico Lebrun, Michael C. McMillen, Ed Moses, James Turrell, and other regional artists. The free gallery and long run make this the most flexible cultural stop in the guide. Allow about an hour, check the city calendar for holiday closures, and pair the exhibition with a directory-linked meal or nearby airport-area stop.

Review the City of Santa Monica's official artists, dates, hours, and gallery context, then browse Santa Monica businesses or compare nearby restaurants.

4. Swiss Winter Village at the Santa Monica Pier: best long-run coastal holiday market

When: November 18-January 3, 2027; check the organizer for daily hours and ticket products. Best for: families, holiday-market shoppers, craft and food explorers, skaters, date nights, and groups that need a long booking window.

Santa Monica's first Swiss Winter Village brings more than 60 chalets, seasonal food, local creations, live entertainment, a Swiss-style market atmosphere, and an ice rink to the Pier. The long run and mix of browsing, food, and skating make it more adaptable than a single tree-lighting night. Admission and activity products are ticketed through the organizer, so compare the exact date and bundle before traveling, budget separately for food and shopping, and use transit when possible during peak holiday evenings.

Review Visit Santa Monica's current November 18-January 3 dates, location, market overview, and official-event link, then browse Santa Monica businesses or compare nearby restaurants.

5. Portraits: Faces and Waves: best free flexible photography stop

When: October 3-March 27, 2027; daily except special events. Best for: photography audiences, Pier visitors, coastal-history readers, portraiture students, and groups needing a quieter alternative to a timed festival.

The Annenberg Community Beach House Gallery pairs Sam Comen's portraits of 274 Santa Monica Pier visitors with Anthony Friedkin's photographs of waves made near the Beach House and State Beach. One body of work records who came to the Pier during America's 250th year, while the other treats individual waves as short-lived forms. It ranks below the one-day programs because it can be visited later, but that flexibility is its advantage. Admission is free; check same-day gallery access around special events, allow 30-60 minutes, and combine the show with the beach or a nearby meal.

Read the City of Santa Monica's official dates, artist context, location, and access note, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

6. Pico Farmers Market: best repeatable food-first morning

When: Saturdays, 8 a.m.-1 p.m. Best for: produce shoppers, picnic planners, families, and visitors who want a low-pressure neighborhood stop.

The Virginia Avenue Park market combines seasonal produce, flowers, bread, cheese, prepared food, and live music in the Pico neighborhood. It ranks below the one-off cultural programs because it is repeatable, but that also makes it the safest fallback if capacity, weather, or timing disrupts another plan. Go early for shopping range or later for lunch energy, and bring a reusable bag plus a separate budget for prepared food.

Confirm the City of Santa Monica's official recurring hours and location, then browse more Santa Monica food options.

Build a practical Santa Monica day

Choose Renegade Craft on November 7-8 for independent makers and food trucks, the November 11 Veterans Day ceremony for a beachside civic program, or the Swiss Winter Village from November 18 for a longer holiday-market and skating window. Use Held in Trust for a free city-collection survey, Portraits: Faces and Waves as a quieter gallery-and-beach plan, or the Pico Farmers Market for a Saturday food-first morning.
