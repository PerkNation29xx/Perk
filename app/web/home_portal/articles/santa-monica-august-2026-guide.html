Santa Monica guide · October 25-November 1

Santa Monica has five current plans built around family fundraising, movies, coastal art, community remembrance, and local food.

Reviewed by Perk Nation Editorial · Updated October 25, 2026 · How we rank and verify recommendations

Party on the Pier brings a ticketed UCLA children's-health fundraiser to Pacific Park on October 25. Cinema by the Sea returns with Practical Magic on October 31, while the Santa Monica Pier's free Día de los Muertos celebration spans October 31-November 1. Portraits: Faces and Waves and the Pico Farmers Market provide flexible free alternatives. The rankings favor distinctiveness, practical value, and how easily each plan extends into a complete Santa Monica day.

1. Party on the Pier: best family fundraiser with rides and play

When: October 25, 10 a.m.-2 p.m. Best for: families, Pacific Park ride fans, supporters of children's health, celebrity-photo audiences, and groups comfortable with premium fundraiser pricing.

UCLA Mattel Children's Hospital's 26th annual fundraiser combines Pacific Park rides, carnival games with prizes, celebrity appearances, photo opportunities, meal vouchers, carnival treats, crafts, a play zone, and UCLA People-Animal Connection therapy dogs. It ranks ahead of the free community programs for the concentration of family activities and its hospital mission, but this is a premium benefit rather than a budget pier day, and carnival games or experiences require separate tickets. Purchase admission before traveling, budget for add-ons, bring a card because the event is cashless, and use transit when possible to avoid pier traffic.

Review the official UCLA event site for the date, hours, activities, cashless policy, transportation note, and tickets, confirm the children's-health fundraising purpose with UCLA Health, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

2. Cinema by the Sea: best outdoor Halloween movie with a coastal setting

When: October 31; seating at 6:30 p.m., film at 7 p.m. Best for: movie-night groups, Halloween date nights, hotel guests, coastal visitors, and audiences wanting a PG-13 classic.

Regent Santa Monica Beach screens Practical Magic outdoors with cocktails and food available. It ranks below the free cultural programs because the tourism listing does not publish admission or reservation details, but the coastal setting offers a relaxed alternative to a crowded street event. Confirm the current booking method, price, seating policy, weather plan, and whether food or drinks are required before traveling; arrive by the posted seating time rather than the film start.

Review Visit Santa Monica's official tourism listing for dates, films, ratings, address, seating time, and venue link, then browse Santa Monica businesses or compare nearby restaurants.

3. Santa Monica Pier Día de los Muertos: best free coastal community altar

When: October 31, 11 a.m.-7 p.m., and November 1, noon-5:30 p.m. Best for: families, cultural learners, pier visitors, public-art audiences, and groups wanting a free program with indoor shelter.

The historic Merry Go-Round building hosts community ofrendas and a tribute to Mexican singer and actor Pedro Infante. Visitors can contribute a copy of a loved one's photograph; the November 1 program adds face painting, an Aztec blessing, folklórico, mariachi, a procession, speeches, and live music. It ranks above a generic pier stop because remembrance and cultural programming are central. Bring copies rather than original photographs, expect pier crowds, and use transit when possible.

Review the Santa Monica Pier's official dates, schedule, altar instructions, artist context, and program, then browse Santa Monica businesses or compare nearby restaurants.

4. Portraits: Faces and Waves: best free flexible photography stop

When: October 3-March 27, 2027; daily except special events. Best for: photography audiences, Pier visitors, coastal-history readers, portraiture students, and groups needing a quieter alternative to a timed festival.

The Annenberg Community Beach House Gallery pairs Sam Comen's portraits of 274 Santa Monica Pier visitors with Anthony Friedkin's photographs of waves made near the Beach House and State Beach. One body of work records who came to the Pier during America's 250th year, while the other treats individual waves as short-lived forms. It ranks below the one-day programs because it can be visited later, but that flexibility is its advantage. Admission is free; check same-day gallery access around special events, allow 30-60 minutes, and combine the show with the beach or a nearby meal.

Read the City of Santa Monica's official dates, artist context, location, and access note, then browse Santa Monica businesses or find a nearby meal.

5. Pico Farmers Market: best repeatable food-first morning

When: Saturdays, 8 a.m.-1 p.m. Best for: produce shoppers, picnic planners, families, and visitors who want a low-pressure neighborhood stop.

The Virginia Avenue Park market combines seasonal produce, flowers, bread, cheese, prepared food, and live music in the Pico neighborhood. It ranks below the one-off cultural programs because it is repeatable, but that also makes it the safest fallback if capacity, weather, or timing disrupts another plan. Go early for shopping range or later for lunch energy, and bring a reusable bag plus a separate budget for prepared food.

Confirm the City of Santa Monica's official recurring hours and location, then browse more Santa Monica food options.

Build a practical Santa Monica day

Choose Party on the Pier on October 25 when a family benefit with rides, games, crafts, and children's-health fundraising fits the budget. Use Portraits: Faces and Waves as a quieter flexible gallery-and-beach plan, the Pico Farmers Market for a Saturday food-first morning, the Practical Magic screening for an October 31 movie night, or the Pier's Día de los Muertos celebration for free community altars and cultural programming.   • Confirm times and weather on the linked official page.
